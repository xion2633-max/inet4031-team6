# Sprint 1 Retrospective

**Owned by:** Scrum Master

This file is completed at the end of week 2. Review what your team shipped, what went well, what went poorly, and one specific process change for Sprint 2.

## What Went Well

Our team worked will together and successfully completed the main tasks for Weeks 1 and 2. We communicated when someone needed help and were able to get our Docker containers running and test database persistence

## What Could Be Better

We could improve our time management. We spent longer than expected finishing some tasks, which caused us to start other parts of the lab later. We could also communicate more consistently about which tasks are completed and which still need work

## What We'll Do Differently Next Sprint

For Sprint 2, we will assign tasks earlier and keep the project board updated as we work. This will help everyone understand their responsibilities and allow us to identify blockers sooner.
