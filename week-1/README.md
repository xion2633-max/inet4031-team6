# Week 1 Lab Files

This directory will contain files you create during Sprint 1 (the synchronous week).

In Week 1, you will:
- Form your team and assign roles
- Create the team charter (this goes in `team-charter.md` at the repo root)
- Set up this GitHub repository
- Initialize your Ansible playbook (in `ansible/` at the repo root)
- Verify access to your shared team container

Week 1 primarily involves documentation and setup rather than infrastructure code. Most of your work is captured in:
- `../README.md` - Team information
- `../team-charter.md` - Team roles and agreements
- `../ansible/` - The initial Ansible playbook
- `../docs/` - Retrospective, environment log, and QA reports

This directory can hold any temporary notes or scripts your team creates during the synchronous session if needed.
